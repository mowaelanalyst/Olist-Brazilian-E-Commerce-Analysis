`Hypotheses & Root Cause Analysis`

* **November Surge  (Black Friday Acceleration):** The primary driver for the November surge was the external market momentum of **Black Friday**, which concentrated massive customer demand and acquisition within a short promotional window (peaking on Nov 24).
* **December Drop  (Natural Baseline Shift, Not a Business Problem):** The December decline was a natural normalization following the holiday rush rather than an operational failure or structural business problem. Sales in December (**$742,184**) remained **+$82,004 higher (+12.4%)** than October’s pre-holiday baseline (**$660,180**), proving the business established a higher permanent performance level post-campaign.

`Summary`

### 1. Performance Overview
* **November Peak:** Sales expanded significantly to **$1,003,862 (+52.1% MoM)**, driven by **7,423 orders (+63.3%)** and **8,628 sold units (+62.8%)**, peaking on **November 24th with 1,166 orders**.
* **December Normalization:** Sales contracted to **$742,184 (-26.1% MoM)**, with orders falling to **5,620 (-24.3%)** and sold units dropping to **6,302 (-27.0%)**.
* **Net Baseline Uplift:** December sales remained **+$82,004 (+12.4%) higher** than the pre-holiday October baseline ($660,180), confirming a higher permanent performance level.

---

### 2. Core Drivers & Observations
* **Acquisition-Driven Expansion:** November growth was **98.29% driven by new customers** (7,190 new buyers out of 7,315), while repeat purchase frequency remained static at **1.18**.
* **Non-Price-Driven Demand:** Growth was not reliant on margin compression; median item price dropped marginally from **$72.0 to $70.0 (-2.8%)**, and AOV fell slightly from **$145.2 to $135.2 (-6.8%)**.
* **Concentration Dynamics:** Growth and pullback were highly symmetrical:
  * **Top States:** São Paulo (`SP`) led November expansion (**37.74%**) and December decline (**-28.93%**), followed by `RJ` and `MG`.
  * **Top Categories:** Home essentials led November unit gains (`Bed Bath Table` **+13.06%**, `Furniture Decor` **+12.18%**) and December contractions (`Bed Bath Table` **-17.91%**, `Furniture Decor` **-16.46%**).
* **Operational Strain:** Logistics friction increased average delivery duration from **11.3 to 14.6 days**, contributing to a minor drop in customer review scores from **4.17 to 3.95**.

---

### 3. Hypotheses
* **November Acceleration:** The revenue spike was propelled by external **Black Friday / Cyber Monday promotional momentum**, concentrating acquisition into a high-volume seasonal window.
* **December Retrenchment:** The month-over-month decline represents a standard post-holiday demand stabilization rather than an operational failure or structural business problem.

---

`What I Learned`

* **Impact of External Factors:** External dynamics—such as Black Friday, seasonal events, and holidays—exert a direct and powerful influence on customer purchasing behavior, often overriding internal product levers.
* **Pricing Is Not the Sole Driver:** High demand surges are not necessarily driven by price cuts. Evaluating data limitations and ruling out internal pricing dynamics is essential to correctly identify external market acceleration.
* **Logistics Capacity Under Strain:** Seasonal demand spikes inevitably test operational boundaries. Managing fulfillment bottlenecks is critical to preserving customer satisfaction and review scores during high-volume campaigns.
